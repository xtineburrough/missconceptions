Paying It Forward

An intervention spreading poetry across Venmo and Instagram to comment on or critique the dehumanizing effects of capitalism. Participants were commissioned and published in a video of ten separate Instagram Reels stitched together to the MIA song, "Paper Planes."

Published Participants

In this project every poet receives a commission. Every poet is published.

Transfer Prints

A series of transfer prints traces the fluid format of transactions, from submitted poem to published intervention. These 6 by 9 inch gessoed hardboards were part of the Art & Code exhibit for DEMO DAYS 2023 at Dunkunsthalle in NYC during NEW INC's summer exhibition showcasing works made by its cohorts.
